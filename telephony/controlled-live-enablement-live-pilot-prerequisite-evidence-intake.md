# Controlled Live Enablement — Live-Pilot Prerequisite Evidence Intake

Stage: QL-051.

QL-051 intakes prerequisite evidence after the post-closure live-pilot readiness decision gate.

It preserves the disabled-only boundary:

- No provider account connection.
- No provider delivery.
- No phone callback runtime.
- No SMS sending.
- No recording.
- No AI drafting or auto-send.
- No persistence writes.
- No live customer access.
- No archive or retention writes.
- No live pilot runtime.

The next step is QL-052, a prerequisite evidence review. QL-052 must not be treated as automatic live enablement.

# QL-234 — Four-Company Held-Pilot Evidence Source Drift, Independent Reassessment Follow-Up Continuity & Conditional Release Integrity Recheck

- Operational mode remains **disabled-preview**. No provider activation, live calls, SMS, real approvals, Supabase writes or automatic hold clearance.
- Scope covers Rosie Dazzlers, Devil n Dove, iCamp and Yard Workers, using isolated session-only organization/scenario ledgers.
- Sources are current, **prepared QL-233** held-pilot audit packets. The QL-233 source evidence, synthetic disposition history and actual QL-181 held state are snapshotted using local-only, non-cryptographic revision markers.
- If the upstream QL-233 packet becomes unprepared or its source identity changes, the downstream QL-234 case freezes. A changed actual hold snapshot freezes it too. Reopening requires explicit case-only confirmation and leaves other organizations unchanged.
- Each fictional gap captures source-match/pending/drift, an unsent independent follow-up continuity draft and unverified conditional-release hold recheck. Open drift, source-pending, inherited hold gaps or a new conditional hold exception prevent local packet preparation.
- Synthetic aging is measured by cycles (0–12), not wall-clock deadlines: two cycles is due soon, three is overdue. After overdue, a fresh independent referral draft is mandatory. Prior overdue episodes survive refresh and recurring drift is counted once per gap per cycle.
- P0 and inherited recurrence/late evidence always require independent follow-up. Changed evidence or a new cycle clears local check acknowledgements so stale acknowledgements cannot prepare a packet.
- A local-only integrity packet requires current evidence, no overdue/due-soon follow-up, no open drift, no missing independent decisions, no integrity exceptions, all gaps reviewed and three UNVERIFIED acknowledgements. Prepared packets are immutable locally.
- The privacy-minimized company receipt exports enum statuses, fictional cycle counts and all twelve actual `HELD_UNVERIFIED` gate states; no free-text notes, fingerprints, contact identity or real evidence.

## Acceptance

1. QL-234 navigation, four-company matrix, contextual help and read-only QL-181 holds render in the operator shell.

5. A fully fictional held case can prepare a privacy-redacted audit receipt, never a real provider dispatch or live-pilot release.

# 13 — Glossary

## Brand

A business workspace inside the shared hub. Initial brands: RosieDazzlers and DevilnDove.

## Contact

A person or organization we communicate with.

## Conversation

A brand-specific thread of communication with a contact.

## Intake request

A structured request from a customer, such as a detailing quote or custom order.

## Message

An inbound, outbound, or internal note attached to a conversation.

## Follow-up task

An action we need to take, usually tied to a contact or conversation.

## Lead quality

Internal signal used to prioritize effort. Never shown to customers.

## AI draft

A message written by AI but not sent until a human approves it.

## Telephony

Phone-related infrastructure: calls, voicemail, routing, recording, SMS, provider events.

## DID

A phone number from a VoIP provider.

## SIP trunk

A phone service connection used by PBX systems like Asterisk/FreePBX.

## PBX

Private Branch Exchange; software or hardware that routes calls.

## SMS inbox

A place to receive/send text messages as business communication.

## Transactional message

A customer-requested or service-related message, such as quote follow-up or booking confirmation.

## Marketing message

A promotional message, such as seasonal offers or newsletters.

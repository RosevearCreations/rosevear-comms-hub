# ADR-0003 — Human Approval Before AI Send

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

AI can help summarize and draft, but customer communication can affect pricing, expectations, trust, and legal/privacy obligations.

## Decision

AI may draft and recommend, but customer-facing AI messages must not send automatically until reviewed and approved by a human.

## Consequences

Positive:

- Lower risk of wrong promises.
- Safer quoting and expectation setting.
- Better brand voice control.

Tradeoffs:

- More manual review.
- Less fully automated than Quo-style AI answering in early stages.

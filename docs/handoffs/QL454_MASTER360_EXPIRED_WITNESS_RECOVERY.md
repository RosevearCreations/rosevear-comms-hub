An exception from signed ON, divergence or other cause must remain escalated, not be silently cleared.
2. **Independent source and roles**: Require protected original host session, independently verified QL-453 custody, trusted clock and fresh nonce/recovery ID, original sensor enrollment and 11 distinct pinned Ed25519 signing keys: original Comms source, independent recovery officer, original Windows host, independent external anchor, renewed physical witness, independent closure authority, three Masters and two separate owners.

Directory permissions 0700, file 0600, fsync both file and parent, canonical digest readback. Missing, corrupt, symlink or conflicting custody holds; interrupted attempt can only resume under a separately authorized supervised recovery.
4. **Original Comms source**: Require fresh signed original OPEN_HOLD case readback binding the exact QL-453 summary, durable final receipt and witness digest; no guessed original host state.
5. **Independently signed recovery outcome**: Distinct officer signs response to original summary and prior witness digest, original expiry status, nonce and fresh timestamp. A prior signed expiry may be `SIGNED_EXPIRED_WITNESS_RECOVERED_UNDER_HOLD` or `SIGNED_RECOVERY_UNRESOLVED_KEEP_HOLD`. 7. **External anchor and newly enrolled physical witness**: Distinct external anchor signs canonical digest of both host samples and source/recovery evidence; monotonic positive sequence and `HISTORICAL_RECOVERY_ONLY_KEEP_OPEN_HOLD`. A separately enrolled physical observer signs the anchor/host digest, original versus new sensor IDs, original Windows machine, receiver ID, independent OFF/ON and physical isolation. Sensor IDs must be different. The independently signed witness must postdate the anchor, have a higher sequence and be **no more than 15 seconds old** at intake and again just before commit.
8. **Classify conservatively**: For an original expiry only independently signed successful remediation **plus** safe host continuity and fresh independent OFF/isolation may produce `SIGNED_HISTORICAL_EXPIRED_WITNESS_RECOVERY_REVIEW_ONLY`. Previously healthy QL-453 may produce `SIGNED_HISTORICAL_HOST_CONTINUITY_RENEWAL_REVIEW_ONLY`. Other QL-453 exceptions, incomplete recovery, signed ON, isolation failure, missing lease or watchdog always yield `SIGNED_WITNESS_RECOVERY_OR_CONTINUITY_EXCEPTION_OPEN` **only if all source evidence is validly signed**; forged, missing, stale or mismatched evidence is HOLD.
9. **Independent authority denies closure**: A separate closure-readiness authority signs exact recovered evidence digest and classification, requires `SEPARATE_OWNER_AND_FRESH_PHYSICAL_OFF_REQUIRED`, OPEN_HOLD and explicit denials of closure/activation/current physical OFF.
10. **Three-Master and dual-owner reconciliation**: Rosevear AI Hub, Rosevear Comms Hub and AI Data Platform each distinctly sign the exact prior receipt, complete recovery evidence and authority digests, classification and corresponding OPEN_HOLD readiness/exception decision. Separate original-source and master owners must sign identical canonical three-Master report digests and keep incident OPEN/HOLD. Missing, replayed or contradictory signatures veto review.
11. **Trusted final clock and durable mutually exclusive result**: Call independently trusted clock again immediately before commit; reject backward/excessive drift, now-expired physical renewal witness, stale authority/owner votes, changed QL-453 private custody or competing QL-454 receipts. Exclusively fsync one REVIEW or EXCEPTION receipt and read back the hash; both statuses retain the physical and owner-controlled closure gate.

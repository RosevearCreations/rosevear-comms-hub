# Rosevear Comms Hub QL-453 — Original Physical OFF Witness Expiry Detection, Reassessment Exception Escalation & Three-Master Closure Gate Renewal

## Policy and non-closure boundary

QL-453 is a historical evidence intake, not a live original-host procedure. A signed physical receiver OFF readback proves **only a specific observation at a specific time**. At any decision point QL-453 keeps `connection=HOLD`, `incidentStatus=OPEN`, `activationAuthorized=false`, `closureAuthorized=false`, `physicalReceiverCurrentlyOff=false`, `manualIsolationAndIndependentOffRequired=true`, and the next-slave gate on HOLD. No software test or previously signed sensor value may change the actual original receiver state.

## Evidence admission and expiry sequence

1. **QL-452 immutable predecessor.** Admit only `DURABLE_QL452_ORIGINAL_HOST_OUTCOME_AUDITED` or `DURABLE_QL452_ORIGINAL_HOST_OUTCOME_EXCEPTION_OPEN`, with correct original classification, owner decision, three-Master count, open status, historical-only physical freshness flag, protected QL-452 PENDING and AUDITED/EXCEPTION receipt hashes and absence of competing outcome. A prior QL-452 exception can never be silently converted to successful closure review.
2. **Separate original/renewed sensor enrollments and original Windows host.** Require independently verified signatures and trusted protected original session for `DESKTOP-AE894KC`; original receiver ID `ISOLATED_COMMS_RECEIVER`; two different explicitly enrolled independent physical sensors; **Eleven distinct pinned Ed25519 signing keys** — original Comms source, original physical witness, original Windows host, external anchor, renewed physical witness, closure boundary authority, Rosevear AI Hub, Rosevear Comms Hub, AI Data Platform, and two distinct owners.
3. **Private one-use custody.** Create an exclusive PENDING receipt for the original incident and new QL-453 renewal ID / nonce distinct from QL-452, write 0600 within 0700 private directory, fsync file and directory and re-read the canonical hash **before** contacting external signers. Reruns, collisions, interruption, or tampering result in HOLD and owner-supervised recovery.
4. **Signed original case and historical witness.** Retrieve fresh OPEN_HOLD original Comms source receipt tied to exact QL-452 outcome summary and durable receipt. Independently verify the historically signed original physical observer's QL-452 outcome and receipt, original host, physical receiver, sensor identifier, OFF/ON, isolation, sequence and timestamp. Do **not** require the original timestamp to be fresh: signed evidence older than 15 seconds is explicitly classified as expired and escalated.
5. **Original host and independent anchor.** Retrieve fresh signed original host OFF/ON readback and isolation-lock, lease-revoked, watchdog flags. Anchor exact original source, original witness and host hashes under a distinct signed custodian, fresh challenge, positive increasing sequence and `SIGNED_WITNESS_HISTORY_ONLY_OPEN_HOLD`.
6. **Independent renewal witness.** Retrieve a *different enrolled sensor ID*, differently signed Ed25519 physical receiver observation with the exact signed original witness, anchor and original-host hashes, fresh challenge, strict monotonic sequence, later timestamp, OFF/ON and independent isolation confirmation. Its signed observation must be at most 15 seconds old. Invalid/forged/out-of-order/missing or already stale **renewal** evidence is HOLD.
7. **Classify, never close.** An original signed witness older than 15 seconds at review, original or renewal ON/divergence, missing isolation control, or QL-452 precursor exception yields `SIGNED_PHYSICAL_OFF_WITNESS_EXPIRED_OR_DIVERGENT_EXCEPTION_OPEN`, even when the newly signed sensor says OFF. Only valid fresh consistent OFF observations and a nonexception QL-452 predecessor yield `SIGNED_PHYSICAL_OFF_WITNESS_REVIEW_FRESH_OPEN_HOLD`. Neither is a physical state assertion now.
8. **Independent closure-gate renewal.** A separately pinned closure authority signs the entire original/renewal/anchor evidence digest and exact healthy/exception classification. It must explicitly forbid connection, activation, physical-current-OFF and incident closure, and require a separate supervised ceremony.
9. **Three distinct Masters and dual owners.** AI Hub, Comms Hub and AI Data Platform each independently sign the QL-452 durable receipt, full witness digest, closure authority digest, exact classification, OPEN_HOLD decision and review time. Two independent source/master owners then sign precisely the same canonical three-Master digest, under distinct keys and IDs. Missing, duplicated, contradictory or stale signatures HOLD.
10. **Final independently trusted clock check.** Read `readTrustedNowMs()` **again immediately before commit**; reject clock regressions, excessive drift and renewed sensor evidence now >15 seconds old, and reject a signed original witness that expired during a previously healthy decision. Do not accept a stale witness as healthy because it was fresh when the run began.
11. **Mutually exclusive durable outcome.** Recheck original QL-452 custody, PENDING receipt and no competing result. Commit exactly one REVIEW or EXCEPTION receipt using 0600 exclusive write/fsync/directory fsync and readback. Durable `DURABLE_QL453_SIGNED_WITNESS_FRESHNESS_REVIEW_ONLY` and `DURABLE_QL453_WITNESS_EXPIRY_EXCEPTION_OPEN` **both** leave original incident OPEN/HOLD with no authorization.

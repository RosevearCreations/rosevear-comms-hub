# QL-455 — Independent Recovery Outcome Freshness Monitoring, Original-Host OFF Exception Recurrence Prevention & Three-Master Ceremony Authority Expiry Governance

## Safety boundary

QL-455 is a historical signed evidence-monitoring module. It does **not** connect or energize DESKTOP-AE894KC, close an incident, assert the receiver is physically OFF **right now**, authorize reactivation, or operate a remote original-host service. Every return keeps `connection=HOLD`, `incidentStatus=OPEN`, `activationAuthorized=false`, `closureAuthorized=false`, `physicalReceiverCurrentlyOff=false`, `manualIsolationAndIndependentOffRequired=true`, and `slaveGate=HOLD_NEXT_MASTER_INTEGRATION_STAGE`. A separately supervised original-host physical/owner ceremony is mandatory for any future state change.

## Protected evidence and expiry sequence

1. **Exact QL-454 predecessor:** Only admit `DURABLE_QL454_WITNESS_RECOVERY_READINESS_REVIEW_ONLY` or `DURABLE_QL454_WITNESS_RECOVERY_EXCEPTION_OPEN` with matching classification, original incident, master count, owner OPEN/HOLD decision, and immutable private QL-454 PENDING plus REVIEW/EXCEPTION receipts. Prior signed exceptions cannot silently heal.
2. **One-use private claim:** Require a fresh monitor ID and nonce different from the predecessor, 12 distinct pinned Ed25519 signer keys (original Comms, independent recovery officer, original Windows host, independent external anchor, independent physical sensor, historical ceremony signer, independent closure gate, three Masters, two owners), protected original host session, independent sensor enrollment and trusted clock. Claim one 0600 PENDING file inside 0700 directory, fsync and verify canonical digest before external reads; replay, symlink and corruption HOLD.
3. **Original Comms and recovery outcome:** Independently signed original Comms source is bound to exact QL-454 summary, recovery digest and protected receipt with OPEN_HOLD. Independently signed recovery officer supplies source-bound recovery status and an explicit short finite validity period (issuedAt/expiresAt), never closure. Signed historical expiry is an exception even if readback currently says OFF.
4. **Original-host OFF recurrence:** Signed fresh original DESKTOP-AE894KC host OFF/ON, isolation lock, revoked lease, watchdog, signed source and recovery digests, monotonic sequence. A signed ON or failed control is an exception, never implicit reactivation.
5. **External anchor and independent OFF/ON witness:** Distinct external Ed25519 anchor binds original source/recovery/host. A separately enrolled sensor signs the same anchor, challenge, original Windows host and isolated receiver ID, distinct sensor IDs, strictly higher sequence, OFF/ON observation and isolation confirmation. It must be no older than 15 seconds at intake and again just before final commit; missing, forged or expired evidence produces HOLD.
6. **Historical ceremony authority expiry:** A separately signed historical ceremony-readiness receipt binds exact recovery digest, same original incident and monitor ID, an issuedAt/expiresAt window (maximum 60 seconds), and explicitly denies closure, activation and current physical OFF. Expired authority is a **signed durable escalation**, not permission to proceed.
7. **Conservative classification:** All of valid original QL-454 historical review, unexpired signed recovery, unexpired signed ceremony readiness, recoveryStatus=RECOVERED_UNDER_HOLD, safe original host OFF/lock/lease/watchdog, and independently signed physical OFF/isolation are required for `SIGNED_RECOVERY_AND_CEREMONY_FRESHNESS_REVIEW_ONLY`. A genuine signed expiry, ON, divergence, unresolved prior exception or failed safeguard produces `SIGNED_RECOVERY_EXPIRY_OR_OFF_RECURRENCE_EXCEPTION_OPEN`. Incomplete, tampered or stale evidence holds.
8. **Independent fail-closed authority:** An independent closure-gate signer explicitly signs exact complete monitor digest, classification, OPEN_HOLD decision and denial of closure/activation/current-OFF. All three distinct Masters sign the same digest and independent gate. Two separate owners then sign identical canonical Master-report digest and keep the incident open.
9. **Final trusted time-of-use check:** Read the independently trusted clock **again** before writing, and reject clock drift, a now-stale physical witness, a newly expired previously healthy recovery or ceremony receipt, changed original QL-454 private custody, or an existing conflicting QL-455 receipt. Commit a single exclusive fsynced REVIEW or EXCEPTION receipt and read back its hash. Successful historical review and durable exception both remain OPEN/HOLD.

# Rosevear Comms Hub QL-452 — Original-Host Recurrence Response Outcome Audit, Independent Physical OFF Evidence Freshness & Three-Master Closure Authority Reverification

## Safety boundary

QL-452 records signed observations of the original Windows host and independently enrolled physical receiver sensor, with explicit clock freshness at **intake**. No software workflow can infer the receiver is physically OFF **now** from an earlier signed observation. All QL-452 outcomes are connection=HOLD, incidentStatus=OPEN, activationAuthorized=false, closureAuthorized=false, physicalReceiverCurrentlyOff=false, and require independent fresh physical isolation verification and a separate explicit, supervised owner authorization ceremony before closure or activation.

## Protected custody and signed evidence review

1. **Exact QL-451 predecessor** — Admit only DURABLE_QL451_ANCHORED_RESPONSE_OFF_RENEWAL_REVIEW_ONLY or DURABLE_QL451_RESPONSE_RECURRENCE_EXCEPTION_OPEN with exact signed historical classification, OPEN/HOLD flags, three-Master count and QL-451 private PENDING + RENEWED/EXCEPTION receipts. Verify immutable receipt SHA and no conflicting outcome.
2. **Trust gates and one-use challenge** — Require trusted original protected DESKTOP-AE894KC session, original QL-451 private custody, separate source/master owners, trusted clock, independently enrolled sensor, independently verified Ed25519 pins and ten distinct signing identities: original Comms source, original Windows host, independent outcome anchor, independent physical receiver witness, independent closure authority, three Masters, two owners. Challenge nonce must differ from QL-451. Claim one-use PENDING in private 0700 directory with 0600 file, fsync and readback before external I/O; interrupted intake requires manual owner recovery and cannot silently replay.
3. **Source and original-host outcome** — Read original Comms signed exact QL-451 summary, response receipt and historical renewal digest, OPEN_HOLD and a fresh timestamp. The original host separately signs exact source digest, event challenge, machine ID, response outcome of either SIGNED_RESPONSE_EFFECTIVE_UNDER_HOLD or SIGNED_RESPONSE_UNRESOLVED_KEEP_HOLD, host reported receiver OFF/ON state, isolation lock, revoked lease, watchdog, positive sequence and later fresh timestamp.
4. **Independent anchored outcome** — A distinct external anchor signs original source and host outcome hashes, original QL-451 receipt, same scoped challenge and incident, unique anchor ID, strictly greater sequence and a later timestamp. Anchor is explicitly HISTORICAL_OUTCOME_ONLY_NO_CLOSURE.
5. **Independent physical receiver observation** — Independently enrolled physical sensor authority signs the exact original host identity, receiver ISOLATED_COMMS_RECEIVER, unique enrolled sensor ID, independent observation type, original host payload and anchor hashes, same incident/nonce, receiver OFF or ON, physical isolation status, strictly greater sequence and observed timestamp. Observation must postdate the anchor and be **no older than 15 seconds at review intake and final commit**; stale, missing or unsigned evidence is HOLD. This observation does not prove the receiver remains OFF after it was measured.
6. **Conservative classification** — Only when original host reports an effective signed response, OFF, protected isolation lock, revoked lease and armed watchdog, AND the independent physical sensor observes OFF with confirmed isolation, classify SIGNED_HISTORICAL_PHYSICAL_OFF_RESPONSE_OUTCOME_AUDITED_OPEN. A signed ON, divergence, unresolved correction or compromised control classifies SIGNED_RESPONSE_OUTCOME_OR_PHYSICAL_OFF_EXCEPTION_OPEN. Both remain OPEN/HOLD; absent or invalid source data fails HOLD.
7. **Separate authority veto** — An independent closure authority signs the full source/host/anchor/physical evidence digest and exact classification, explicitly rejecting physicalReceiverCurrentlyOff=true, closureAuthorized=true, activationAuthorized=true and anything other than OPEN_HOLD. Authority disposition requires a future supervised physical ceremony; never use this review as final closure authority.
8. **Three Master and dual-owner verification** — Rosevear AI Hub, Rosevear Comms Hub, and AI Data Platform each separately sign exact prior receipt, outcome digest, independent boundary hash, classification, OPEN_HOLD decision and fresh review time. Two distinct source/master owners then sign the identical three-Master digest, authority veto, case and OPEN_HOLD historical-only decision. Duplicate, missing, stale or contradictory votes fail HOLD.
9. **Private immutable outcome** — Before completion, recheck original QL-451 custody, one-use PENDING, independent physical sensor freshness <=15s, all signers and absence of conflicting receipts. Commit exactly one AUDITED or EXCEPTION receipt via exclusive fsynced 0600 file, directory fsync, then readback hash. Stable evidence is historical-only; exceptions require supervised original-host remediation and a new independent observation.

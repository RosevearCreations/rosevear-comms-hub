Both successful review and signed recurrence exception remain connection=HOLD, activationAuthorized=false, closureAuthorized=false, physicalReceiverCurrentlyOff=false, incidentStatus=OPEN, manualIsolationAndIndependentOffRequired=true and slaveGate=HOLD_NEXT_MASTER_INTEGRATION_STAGE.

## Protected flow

1. **Exact predecessor.** Accept QL-450's DURABLE_QL450_POST_DISPOSITION_OFF_STABLE_REVIEW_ONLY or DURABLE_QL450_POST_DISPOSITION_RECURRENCE_EXCEPTION_OPEN only with their exact classification, owner decision, original incident ID, historical stability/boundary digests, three-Master count, and matching PENDING plus STABLE or EXCEPTION private receipt. Reject contradictory custody or closed cases.
2. **Original-host trust.** Require independently verified protected Windows host session on DESKTOP-AE894KC, original QL-450 private receipts, trusted clock, independently verified pins and eleven distinct Ed25519 signer keys: original Comms source, independent response officer, independent external anchor, original Windows host, independent receiver witness, independent closure boundary, three Masters and two owners.
3. **One-use protective claim.** A new QL-451 renewal ID and nonce different from the predecessor's are atomically claimed in an exclusive 0600 PENDING file under a 0700 directory before external reads; fsync file and directory, re-read exact canonical hash. Replayed, interrupted or tampered custody is HOLD and requires supervised recovery, never implicit retry.
4. **Open original Comms case readback.** Verify original-source signature over exact QL-450 summary, predecessor STABLE/EXCEPTION receipt hash, QL-450 stability digest, prior classification, OPEN_HOLD and fresh source time.
5. **Independent corrective response.** Accept an independently signed remediation officer's scoped response with exact source, QL-450 receipt and nonce. For a QL-450 recurrence, the response must explicitly say either SIGNED_RESPONSE_COMPLETED_UNDER_HOLD or SIGNED_RESPONSE_UNRESOLVED_KEEP_HOLD. Previously stable cases may report NO_NEW_RECURRENCE_SIGNED_RENEWAL. Signed isolation-lock, revoked-lease and watchdog booleans must also be verified; an incomplete response cannot produce success.
6. **Independently anchored response.** Obtain distinct external anchor signature binding the exact original Comms source, independent corrective response payload digest, fresh nonce, QL-450 original receipt and positive sequence. Its disposition is HISTORICAL_RESPONSE_ONLY_NO_INCIDENT_CLOSURE, never closure permission.
7. **Renewed original-host OFF/ON samples.** Require SHORT, MEDIUM at least 10 seconds after SHORT and LONG at least 30 seconds after SHORT signed original DESKTOP-AE894KC observations. Every host receipt signs the exact QL-450 receipt, correction, anchor, nonce, review, horizon, monotonic sequence and previous independent witness digest. Validate observed OFF or ON, isolation lock, lease revoked and watchdog status, and fresh ordered timestamps.
8. **Separate independent signed witnesses.** Every horizon must also have independent receiver observer signature binding the host payload hash, same anchor and previous chain digest, later timestamp, strictly greater sequence, observed OFF/ON and independent isolation confirmation. A signed ON, disagreement, false control or incomplete response makes a durable recurrence exception. Missing forged or stale evidence is HOLD.
9. **Independent closure boundary.** The distinct boundary authority signs the exact historical renewal digest, classification, and explicit denials of closure, activation and a current physical OFF assertion, keeping OPEN_HOLD and OWNER_GATED_REASSESSMENT_ONLY_SEPARATE_CLOSURE_CEREMONY.
10. **Three-Master and two-owner reassessment.** Distinct Ed25519 votes from Rosevear AI Hub, Rosevear Comms Hub and AI Data Platform all bind QL-450 receipt, signed historical OFF renewal digest, independent boundary, case, reassessment, classification and OPEN_HOLD. Separate source and master owners sign exactly the same Master-report digest and denial-of-closure outcome.
11. **Private durable outcome.** Re-read source QL-450 protected receipts, QL-451 PENDING and freshness, exclude concurrent/conflicting outcomes, then exclusive fsync either RENEWED historical-only or EXCEPTION receipt. Read the exact receipt hash back. Successful repaired QL-450 exception may set responsePreconditionsEvidenced=true, **not** closureAuthorized or physicalReceiverCurrentlyOff. Review requires a future separate physical and owner-supervised ceremony.

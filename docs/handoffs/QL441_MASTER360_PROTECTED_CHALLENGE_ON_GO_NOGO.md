# QL-441 — Protected Fresh-Ceremony Challenge Consumption, Dual-Owner Runtime Authority Separation & Independently Witnessed Receiver ON Go/No-Go

## Scope and safety boundary

QL-440 issued authority to **attempt** a new original-host supervised ceremony, not to turn a receiver ON. QL-441 is a **read-only verifier of a separately authorized, bounded isolated ON witness**, not a command to activate, retain, or connect the receiver. Nothing in this code actuates the hardware, enrolls real signing keys, issues customer/provider messages or promotes the six application shells to CONNECTED. **All results retain** `connection=HOLD`, `activationAuthorized=false`, `slaveGate=HOLD_NEXT_MASTER_INTEGRATION_STAGE` and `manualReceiverIsolationRequired=true`.

The positive `GO_WITNESS_ACCEPTANCE_ONLY` result means **signed, time-bounded ON-observation evidence was accepted for review**. It does not authorize continued operation of a receiver. The `NO_GO_SHUTDOWN_REQUIRED` result indicates that at least one Master issued a signed veto after independent ON observation and demands prompt original-host shutdown and independent OFF confirmation. All malformed, missing, stale, duplicated, unauthorized or untrusted inputs fail closed to HOLD; when a receiver might be ON and trust is lost, treat isolation and fresh OFF as **operator actions requiring a real signed witness**, not assumptions inferred from test reports.

3. Require **distinct independently verified** signed `source` and `master` owner approval envelopes. Each owner uses a personal pinned Ed25519 key and a distinct identity, and signs only `AUTHORIZE_BOUNDED_ISOLATED_ON_OBSERVATION_ONLY_NO_CONNECT`. Authentication/MFA or a shared account is not an owner approval; one owner cannot approve both roles.
4. Before reading external evidence, durably claim one-use `PENDING` custody for the original incident in a protected absolute directory (0700), with 0600 exclusive create, file/directory fsync and exact readback. Interrupted custody is non-replayable; do not auto-resume an ON ceremony.
5. Independently obtain the actual QL-440 **original-host signed issuance envelope**, verify the pinned issuer signature and exact previously recorded issuance payload digest. This rejects any unauthenticated assertion that a code-green QL-440 had performed live signing.
6. Obtain fresh **signed challenge consumption on DESKTOP-AE894KC**, bound to QL-440 issuer digest, both separately signed owner approval digests, challenge/nonce/lease, armed watchdog, positive consumption sequence and expiry. Signatures from TEMPLAR or a reused/personal-operator key fail closed.
7. A separate authorized external original-host observer reports signed `ON` within the lease, a still-armed watchdog, and an increasing ON sequence. A **separately pinned independent receiver observer** must read and sign `ON` no earlier than the original-host witness, with nondecreasing sequence and exact host ON payload digest. This is an *observation*, not an ON command supplied by QL-441.
8. Three **independently signed Master decisions**, from `rosevear-ai-hub`, `rosevear-comms-hub` and `ai-data-platform`, must bind exact challenge and independent ON digest. Values are limited to `GO_WITNESS_ACCEPTANCE_ONLY_NO_CONNECT` or `NO_GO_SHUTDOWN_REQUIRED`. Missing, stale, duplicate and ambiguous decisions fail closed. 9. After all three Master decisions, both distinct original owners must sign the **same final bounded review decision**. For GO, `DUAL_OWNER_GO_ACCEPT_WITNESSED_ON_AND_REQUIRE_SHUTDOWN`; for NO_GO, `DUAL_OWNER_NO_GO_REQUIRE_IMMEDIATE_SHUTDOWN`. Both bind exact separate first-phase owner signatures, independent ON digest and canonical three-Master digest.
10. Re-read protected QL-440 and QL-441 pending custody, revalidate lease still unexpired, and atomically write a one-use fsynced `DECISION` receipt. The only next permitted action is **separately signed isolated shutdown and independent OFF reconciliation**; no CONNECTED transition is made by this build.
11. On subsequent boot, a private decision receipt returns `QL441_PRIVATE_DECISION_FRESH_OFF_AND_LEASE_CLOSEOUT_REQUIRED` and HOLD. It cannot prove the physical receiver is still ON, OFF, or safely shut down. If readback is lost, stop and require witnessed isolation.

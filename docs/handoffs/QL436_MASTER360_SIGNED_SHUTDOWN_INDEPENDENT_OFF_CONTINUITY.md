This release cannot claim live receiver shutdown or actual CONNECTED.

All outcomes preserve **connection=HOLD**, **activationAuthorized=false**, **slaveGate=HOLD_NEXT_MASTER_INTEGRATION_STAGE**, **manualReceiverIsolationRequired=true**. When original-host shutdown evidence or independent OFF evidence is absent or invalid, the receiver status is **UNKNOWN_UNTIL_INDEPENDENT_SIGNED_OFF**, not OFF. Even a durable accepted signed OFF review does not authorize restarting the pilot or promoting all six applications.

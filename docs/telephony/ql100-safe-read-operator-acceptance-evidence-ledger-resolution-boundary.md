# Telephony Boundary — QL-100

QL-100 does not change telephony behavior.

## Disabled in QL-100

- Phone/SMS sending.
- Provider callbacks.
- Callback registration.
- Live calls.
- Recordings.
- SMS/MMS content persistence.
- Consent writes.
- Archive writes.
- Audit/admin writes.
- Retention writes.
- AI generation or send.
- Live pilot runtime.

The Safe Reads panel remains a public disabled preview with bounded, synthetic safe-read evidence only.

## Scope and outcome
QL-129 produces a **display-only, synthetic/public safe-read follow-up plan** from QL-128's handoff readiness review. A passing result permits only a separate future planning review, **not** owner approval, consent, implementation, or live operator communications. Failed prerequisites produce `follow_up_plan_revision_required`.

## Eight fail-closed planning gates
1. Exactly eight unique passing QL-128 review gates with expected identities.
2. Recomputed source summary and QL-128 outcome match.
3. All owner, implementation, schema, API, browser and ledger write locks remain closed.
4. Synthetic/public packet, questions, handoff prerequisites and review agenda remain available.
5. Owner decisions, signatures, consent and captures remain deferred.
6. Private/customer/provider data are excluded from candidate planning fields.
7. Phone/SMS, DTMF, provider callbacks, recording, AI delivery, pilot and persistence remain disabled.
8. Future QL-130 review, owner authorization, stop conditions and independent release checks remain mandatory.

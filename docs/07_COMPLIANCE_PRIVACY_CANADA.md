# 07 — Compliance and Privacy Notes for Canada

## Purpose

This file is a practical checklist, not legal advice.

## Core privacy principles

Collect only what is needed, explain why, keep it secure, and delete it when no longer needed.

## Call recording

Before recording calls or transcribing voicemails/calls, the system should:

- notify the caller that recording may occur
- explain the reason, such as quality, quote accuracy, or follow-up
- provide an option to continue or leave a message
- store consent evidence where practical
- restrict access to recordings/transcripts
- define retention and deletion rules

Do not enable call recording in QL-001.

## AI processing

Before sending call audio, transcripts, or customer messages to an AI provider, document:

- what data is sent
- why it is sent
- where it is stored
- who can access it
- whether it is retained
- how customers can request deletion where applicable

## SMS and email categories

Separate transactional messages from marketing messages.

### Transactional messages

Examples:

- quote follow-up
- booking confirmation
- appointment reschedule
- photo request
- deposit/payment reminder tied to a requested service

### Marketing messages

Examples:

- seasonal promotions
- newsletters
- sales campaigns
- general offers not directly tied to a customer request

Marketing needs stricter consent and unsubscribe handling.

## Consent logs

Store consent events for:

- SMS communication
- email communication
- marketing communication
- call recording
- voicemail transcription
- AI processing if needed

## Sensitive information

Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive details.

Customers may share personal stories, health concerns, household details, vehicle contents, addresses, or personal circumstances. Store only what is needed to complete the service.

## Address/location handling

For RosieDazzlers, addresses may be needed for mobile service. Keep address access limited and do not expose it unnecessarily.

For DevilnDove, shipping/pickup details may be needed. Keep them linked to orders, not casual conversations when possible.

## Data retention draft

Initial retention idea:

- Active leads: keep while active.
- Completed jobs/orders: keep business records as needed.
- Call recordings: avoid storing at first; later use short retention unless needed for dispute or training.
- AI drafts: keep only as long as useful for conversation history.
- Marketing consent logs: keep while consent status matters.

## Approval requirements before live phone/SMS

Before live phone/SMS integration:

- customer-facing greeting/consent text drafted
- SMS opt-out wording drafted
- internal access rules decided
- data retention settings decided
- test number used before existing number forwarding/porting

Use this checklist only for the public disabled preview review.

1. Open the disabled preview and open the Safe Reads panel.
2. Select Rosie Dazzlers and Devil n Dove separately.
3. Confirm the QL-096 review summary is visible.
4. Confirm the QL-095 acceptance plan remains visible under review.

6. Confirm rejected rows are summarized by count and bounded reason only.

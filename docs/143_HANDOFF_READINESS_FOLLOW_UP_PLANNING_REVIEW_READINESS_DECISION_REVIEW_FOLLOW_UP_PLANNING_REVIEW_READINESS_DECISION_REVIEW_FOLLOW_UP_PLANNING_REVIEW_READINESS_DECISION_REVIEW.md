## Scope and safeguards
QL-140 independently **reviews the QL-139 synthetic/public planning readiness decision**, permitting only a later separately scoped synthetic/public follow-up planning step. No real owner acceptance/consent, evidence persistence, database/API/browser write or live Phone/SMS/provider/recording/AI send/pilot is enabled.

## Eight fail-closed review checks
1. Exactly eight unique expected QL-139 gate IDs with passing statuses.
2. Independently recomputed QL-139 pass/review/blocked counts reconcile with its declared summary and outcome.
3. Owner, implementation, consent and schema/API/Worker/Supabase/browser/ledger write permissions remain closed.
4. Read-only synthetic/public owner packet, agenda, questions and handoff prerequisites stay available.
5. Owner decisions, consent, signatures and real approval remain uncollected and separately gated.
6. Restricted private customer/provider fields remain excluded from planning candidates.
7. Phone/SMS, DTMF, provider callbacks, recordings, AI delivery, pilot and persistence remain disabled.
8. A distinct QL-141 follow-up, real owner authority and exact-final-main release verification remain required.

Any failed gate produces `readiness_decision_review_revision_required`. Passing yields only `ready_for_separate_synthetic_follow_up_planning_only`, not owner approval or implementation rights.
